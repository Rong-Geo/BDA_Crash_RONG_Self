Metrics
Frequency counts unique collision reports (distinct report_id) per police beat and year. The serious/fatal rate divides participants with serious/severe or fatal injuries by participants with known injury status, shown only for beat-years with at least 30 known injury records.
Classes
Classes are relative quintiles of all 2018–2025 beat-years (zero is a separate class for sparse fatal metrics). The breaks are shared across years, so colors are comparable over time.
  • The maps show observed patterns, not exposure-adjusted risk or causal danger.
  • Beats with no record in a year are grey (No collision summary); 2016–2017 are excluded because their records are incomplete.
  • Injury status is unknown for most participant records; rates describe the known subset only.
